Files / If it happens to you

Report it quickly

Speed matters. The sooner a fraud is reported, the better the chance that the money can be stopped. You are the victim, not the guilty one, so do not feel ashamed to report.

National cyber crime helpline

1930

Call as soon as you notice a fraud. Then file the complaint on the national portal, cybercrime.gov.in.

Open portal
  1. Call 1930 right awayShare the transaction ID, amount, time and the scammer's number or account.
  2. Tell your bank or UPI appAsk them to block the card, freeze the account and flag the transaction.
  3. Save the evidenceScreenshots, chats, call numbers, links, receipts. Do not delete anything.
  4. File the complaint onlineRegister on cybercrime.gov.in with the evidence attached and keep the acknowledgement number.
  5. Secure your accountsChange passwords, log out of all devices, switch on 2-step verification.
  6. Tell someone you trustFamily and friends can help, and may be the next target of the same scammer.
